# Wavelet

A free music player for your phone. It searches YouTube and plays songs through YouTube's official player.

- Open the site on your phone and use "Add to Home Screen" to install it like an app.
- Search needs a free YouTube Data API v3 key (Settings in the app explains how to get one). The key stays on your phone.
- Your liked songs and playlists are saved on your phone only. Use Settings > Back up your library to keep a copy.
