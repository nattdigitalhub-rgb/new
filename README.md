# Wavelet

A free music player for your phone. It searches YouTube and plays songs through YouTube's official player.

- Open the site on your phone and use "Add to Home Screen" to install it like an app.
- Two sources: YouTube (official player, needs a free YouTube Data API v3 key, stops when the screen locks) and Jamendo (free Creative Commons indie music, needs a free Jamendo Client ID, keeps playing when locked). Settings in the app explains both.
- Favorites, playlists, albums and followed artists are saved on your phone only. Use Settings > Back up your library to keep a copy.
