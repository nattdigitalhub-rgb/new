YouTube search key

This key stays on this phone. It is never sent anywhere except to YouTube.

How to get a free key

  1. Open Google Cloud and sign in with any Google account. Create a project (any name, like "Wavelet").
  2. Open the YouTube Data API v3 page and tap Enable.
  3. Go to Credentials, tap Create credentials, then API key. Copy the key.
  4. Paste it above and tap Save key.

It's free with no card needed. The free allowance covers about 100 searches a day. Playing songs doesn't use it up.

Jamendo (plays with the screen locked)

Jamendo has over 600,000 free songs from independent artists, shared legally under Creative Commons. They keep playing when your phone is locked. To search it, you need a free Client ID.

  1. Open the Jamendo developer site and make a free account.
  2. Go to My Apps and tap Add a new app. Name it "Wavelet". For the website, use this app's address.
  3. Copy the Client ID it shows (a short mix of letters and numbers).

Back up your library

Your favorites, playlists, albums and artists are saved on this phone only. Copy a backup to keep them safe or move them to another phone.

Paste a backup above, then tap Restore.

YouTube songs play through YouTube's official player, so YouTube ads may appear and they stop when your screen locks. Jamendo songs keep playing.
